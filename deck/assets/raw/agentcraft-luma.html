About Event

​Agent Craft is an inspiring, practical and interactive event for agent builders.

​On Friday 12th June we wrap up London Tech week in style, gathering 100 Agent Builders in Shoreditch, creating space to learn from industry leaders and from each other.

​Who should attend?

  • ​You're building AI Agents

  • ​You want to learn how to build AI Agents

  • ​You're transforming your business with AI Agents

  • ​You want advice on security and reliability for AI Agents

​The Day

​We spend the morning hearing from inspiring keynote speakers with incredible demos of agents in action.

​Then in the afternoon it's over to you. You drive the agenda. What do you want to talk about? What are you building? What challenges are you facing?

  1. ​Pitch your topic

  2. ​Vote for your favourites

  3. ​Start the discussion

​The unconference format in the afternoon is the ultimate networking and peer to peer learning opportunity. Whether there are 30 or just 3 people in your session, you know you've found your people. The people who care about the same problems as you.

​The full agenda for the day is shared on our website.

​Topics We Expect to Cover

​OpenClaw | Agent Security | Multi-Agent Teams | Context Engineering | Agent Orchestration | MCP | A2A | LangChain | Reliability | Claude Code | AgentKit | Agentic Coding
